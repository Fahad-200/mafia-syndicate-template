Common inquiries

How do I become a member?

Membership is by invitation only. If you possess the influence, capital, or strategic value we seek, our council will reach out. Unsolicited applications are not accepted.

What industries do you serve?

We operate across finance, technology, defense, energy, media, and private equity. Our network spans industries where leverage and discretion are paramount.

How does pricing work?

Engagements are structured as retainers with performance-based incentives. We align our interests with yours — when you win, we win. Initial consultation is complimentary for qualified parties.

Is my confidentiality guaranteed?

Absolute discretion is the foundation of our operation. All engagements are protected by multi-jurisdictional NDAs and our internal code of silence. Your affairs remain your own.

What is the minimum engagement?

We do not operate on a project basis. All engagements begin with a minimum 12-month retainer, renewable by mutual consent. This ensures alignment of interests and allows our teams to execute multi-phase strategies without artificial time constraints.

Do you work with startups?

We selectively engage with high-potential ventures that demonstrate exceptional strategic value. Our incubation division identifies and backs 3-5 early-stage companies annually, providing capital, network access, and operational infrastructure in exchange for strategic equity positions.

How do you handle conflicts of interest?

Our internal charter strictly prohibits representing competing interests within the same sector. Before any engagement, we conduct a thorough conflict scan across our entire portfolio. If a conflict is identified, we refer potential clients to our trusted network of allied firms.
